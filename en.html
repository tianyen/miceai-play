BEFORE WE PLAY

Before we play.
A few useful answers.

Can we use our branding and custom questions?

We can plan around your brand identity, event theme, product content and questions. Customization, revision rounds and deliverables are agreed during the proposal stage.

What can a digital drop deliver?

Options include promotional passes, experience invitations and branded content. Timing, eligibility and access are planned around the event goal and system setup.

Where can a pass take participants?

Passes can direct participants to store information, ticketing, experience bookings or a chosen brand page. External integrations, redemption and tracking require a separate scope review.

What equipment and audience size can we plan for?

Requirements depend on the game, concurrent audience, devices and venue network. Share your event size and venue details so we can assess the setup and testing needed.

How are pricing and lead times determined?

Pricing and lead time depend on the games, customization, pass requirements, equipment and on-site support. Share your date, goals and available assets to start planning.

Do these demos collect personal information?

The quiz, poll, message wall and pass demo run only in your browser session. No valid passes are issued. The inquiry form creates a local brief for you to review and send yourself.
